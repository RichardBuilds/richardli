The Problem
Most scaling businesses are running on infrastructure built for a smaller version of themselves.

It usually doesn't announce itself. Things just get harder. The founder ends up in more meetings, not fewer. Delivery becomes unpredictable. Good people leave because nobody has defined what good actually looks like.

The systems that got the business here were built for a different size. The spreadsheets, the group chats, the founder as the final answer to everything. They worked then. Now they're the ceiling.

This is not a people problem. It's an infrastructure problem. And it's fixable.
